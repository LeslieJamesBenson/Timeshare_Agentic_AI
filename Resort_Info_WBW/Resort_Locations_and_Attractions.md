# WorldMark by Wyndham (WBW) — Resort Locations & Top Attractions

A reference for every resort in the `Resort_Info_WBW` folder. For each resort you'll find its
location (city / area) and 2–3 of the biggest tourist attractions or things to do nearby.

> **Note on data:** The resort JSON files only store `resort` name and `state`. Locations below
> were identified from the WorldMark/Wyndham property listings for each resort. A few genuinely
> ambiguous cases were verified against the official Wyndham resort pages (flagged inline).

---

- **Banff National Park & Lake Louise** — turquoise glacial lakes, the Banff Gondola, and hot springs.
- **Icefields Parkway** — one of the world's most scenic drives (Columbia Icefield, Peyto Lake).

## Arizona

- **Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest** — hiking, fishing, and camping among the pines.
