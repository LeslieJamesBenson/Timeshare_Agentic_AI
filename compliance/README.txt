COMPLIANCE DOCUMENTS FOLDER
============================
Drop Travel + Leisure / Wyndham-specific compliance and legal guidelines here.
Supported formats: .docx, .pdf, .txt

The sales AI agent currently operates under standard US timeshare regulations
(built into its system prompt). Documents added here will allow the agent to
incorporate company-specific rules, required disclosures, and prohibited language.

Examples of what to upload:
  - TL-sales-compliance-guidelines.pdf
  - required-disclosures-by-state.docx
  - prohibited-language-list.txt
  - rescission-period-rules.pdf
  - FTC-timeshare-guidelines.pdf

Standard guardrails already built in (no document needed):
  - No investment/appreciation language
  - No guaranteed rental income claims
  - No misrepresentation of rescission period
  - No high-pressure closing language
  - No false statements about exchange availability
  - Required disclosure of all ongoing fees (maintenance, HOA, etc.)
